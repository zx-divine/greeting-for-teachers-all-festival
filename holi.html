GREETING VIA MANURAJ PANDEY

Happy Holi! 🎨🌈

Respected Mam,

Holi is the festival of colors, and thinking about it, you've added quite a few colors to our lives that we didn't have before — the color of curiosity when a topic finally clicked, the color of confidence after you told us we could do better and meant it.

It's also a festival about letting go — of old grudges, old mistakes, old versions of ourselves. You've taught more than one of us how to do exactly that: to mess up, learn, and move forward without carrying the weight of it.

A classroom is honestly a lot like a Holi crowd — loud, colorful, a little chaotic, and somehow all the better for it. Thank you for never trying to mute that, only to guide it.

This Holi, may your life be filled with as much color, laughter, and sweetness as you've brought into ours.

Happy Holi, Mam — bura na maanna, but we really do owe you more thanks than we usually say out loud.

Happy Independence Day! 🇮🇳🎉

Respected Mam,

Independence Day reminds us of the freedom an entire nation fought for, but there's a quieter kind of freedom you've given us too — the freedom to ask questions without feeling small, and to be wrong in your classroom without being made to feel it.

A country isn't built only in its parliaments; it's built one classroom at a time, by teachers who take the trouble to shape not just students, but citizens who can think for themselves. That's exactly what you've done for us.

Every value you've quietly taught — fairness, discipline, respect for people different from us — is the same thread that holds a free country together. You've handed us more of that than any textbook chapter could.

On this Independence Day, thank you for being one of the people actually doing the work of nation-building, one lesson at a time.

Jai Hind, Mam — and thank you.

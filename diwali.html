Respected Mam,

On this Diwali, I want to pause and say thank you — because long before we knew what a diya was for, you were already lighting one for us, one lesson at a time.

A festival of lights makes so much more sense once you've had a teacher like you. You've shown us that knowledge is its own kind of flame: it doesn't reduce when it's shared, it only spreads further, brighter, into rooms it hasn't reached yet.

Every doubt you cleared, every mistake you corrected gently instead of harshly, every extra minute you gave us after the bell had already rung — that was you lighting a diya in a corner we didn't even know was dark.

This Diwali, may your home be as full of warmth as our classroom is because of you. May the coming year bring you the same peace, prosperity, and joy that you've always tried to bring into ours.

Thank you for being one of the steadiest lights in our lives, on Diwali and on every ordinary day in between.
